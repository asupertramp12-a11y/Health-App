Data and backups

Case data

Choose your case-data.json. It stays in this browser on this device only, and is never uploaded.

Your notes

Questions, ticks, notes, diary entries and appointments you add are saved in this browser only. Back them up to keep them safe or move them between phone and laptop.

Add to my notes keeps everything on this device and adds anything new from the backup. Replace swaps your notes for the backup; a copy of your current notes downloads first.

Remove or clear data

Use these only when you mean to start again on this device. Clearing your notes downloads a backup first.
